# Screenplay, storyboard and shot list (main film, 2:20, 16:9, 24 fps)

## How to read this file
- **G** = generated with Higgsfield Kling 3.0 Pro (image-to-video from an approved start frame, silent, 1080p).
- **A** = real app recording (video-production/footage/app), composited into a device or a clean stage.
- **T** = deterministic title or motion graphics (edit scripts).

**Start frames** are made first with `gpt_image_2_5` from the reference sheet (references/), so the CEO, Knot and the office stay consistent.

**Global negative constraints** (append to every G prompt): no readable text or UI on screens (screens show soft abstract glow only), no logos except where stated, no extra limbs, no warping faces, no flicker, no camera shake, no cartoon outlines on humans.

**Look tokens** (append to every G prompt): photoreal cinematic film still, anamorphic 2.39 feel within a 16:9 frame, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric haze, warm tungsten practicals and cool window light, teal-amber grade, fine film grain.

## SCENE 1: The tangled thought (0:00–0:12)

**G01** · 5 s · start frame: R1 on black
> Total darkness. A faint glow pulses, and a small creature made of tangled black scribble threads with colour flecks slowly unfurls in mid-air: two huge pale green eyes blink open, curious. Thin luminous threads drift off it like loose thoughts. Slow push-in, very shallow depth of field. Mysterious and playful.

- **Transition:** match-cut on the eyes into G02.
- **VO (0:02):** "Every business begins with an idea."

**G02** · 5 s · start frame: R3 with Knot small in the foreground
> Dusk, light rain. The little thread creature floats past wet street lights toward a brick-and-glass office building with warm windows. Camera tracks behind it at shoulder height, slow and smooth, reflections on the pavement.

- **VO (0:07):** "But somewhere along the way… things get complicated."

## SCENE 2: Exploring the company (0:12–0:27)

**G03** · 5 s · start frame: R4 + Knot
> Inside a busy small office. The thread creature drifts over a front desk buried in paper invoices and binders. An employee stamps papers, another searches a drawer for a missing folder. The creature peeks into an open binder; a loose sheet flutters up. Steadicam follow, left to right.

**G04** · 5 s · start frame: R5 + Knot
> A wall covered with hand-written staff schedules, crossed-out names and sticky notes. A woman rewrites a rota by hand. The creature brushes a sticky note; it falls; she sighs and sticks it back. Slow lateral dolly.

**G05** · 5 s · start frame: R6 + Knot
> A finance corner: a man switches rapidly between two monitors glowing with abstract green spreadsheet grids, papers everywhere. The creature hides between the monitors, eyes darting left and right with each switch. Slow push-in.

- **Sound:** office ambience, paper, typing, a phone ringing.
- **VO:** silent (let the visuals play).

## SCENE 3: Finding the owner (0:27–0:42)

**G06** · 5 s · start frame: R7 with Daniel at his desk + Knot in the doorway
> Evening. In a glass office a tired business owner rubs his eyes over a desk buried in folders. The thread creature floats in through the open door and hovers closer, curious. Slow push-in to a medium close-up.

**G07** · 5 s · start frame: close-up of Daniel + Knot near his temple · **hero shot**
> Extreme close-up: the thread creature dives straight into the man's temple. Inside his mind, luminous tangled threads, scattered task cards, crossing arrows and red question marks multiply and spin. Cinematic VFX, dark background with glowing threads, fast but readable.

**G08** · 5 s · start frame: R12-chaos (Knot on Daniel's head)
> Pull back: the little creature now sits on top of the man's head, arms folded, like a tiny anxious hat. He sighs and leans back. Behind the glass, the office looks even busier. Slow dolly out.

- **VO (0:38):** "It isn't the people. It's everything that doesn't connect."

## SCENE 4: He shows how it works (0:42–1:05)

**G09** · 10 s · start frame: Daniel standing on the open floor, Knot on his head
> The owner stands up and walks through the open-plan office, talking and gesturing as if explaining to someone beside him: he points at the front desk papers, then the schedule wall. Smooth Steadicam walking shot, slightly behind and beside him. The creature on his head reacts with each problem: covers its eyes, shrugs.

**G10** · 5 s · start frame: Daniel at R5
> At the schedule wall, he taps a crossed-out name and explains with his hands; the creature on his head points too. Medium shot, gentle push-in.

**G11** · 5 s · start frame: Daniel at R6
> At the finance corner he leans over the two glowing monitors and explains, counting on his fingers; the creature on his head looks dizzy. Over-the-shoulder.

- **VO (0:48):** "Different tools. Different files. Endless workarounds."

**G12** · 5 s · start frame: reverse angle, Daniel holding a phone up, recording · **the reveal**
> Reverse angle: we see he has been holding his phone up the whole time, recording the office as he explains. He lowers it, looks at the screen, a small hopeful smile. The creature on his head leans over to look at the phone too. Shallow focus on the phone edge.

- **Phone screen:** blank glow only. The real UI is composited in the edit from A02.
- **VO (0:58):** "What if your business could simply show the system how it works?"

## SCENE 9: Knot transforms (2:16–2:23) · hero

**G16** · 5 s · start frame: R12 (calm Daniel, Knot on his head) · end frame: R11 (the 3D logo on dark)
> Close on the calm owner; the camera tilts up to the little creature on his head. It freezes, looks around, puzzled, then down at itself: its tangled threads begin to straighten and slide into six clean aperture blades of cyan and violet light; it gives a surprised little grin as its body becomes a glossy camera-aperture logo, which lifts off and glows. Continuous, one take.
