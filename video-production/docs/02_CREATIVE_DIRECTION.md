# Creative direction

**Line:** *Your business shouldn't have to adapt to software. Your software should adapt to your business.*
**Arc:** Chaos → Frustration → Realization → Solution → System creation → Control → Transformation.
**Length:** 2:20 (main), 16:9, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Cut-downs: 60 s, 30 s and 9:16 later, only if budget remains.

## Two worlds
| | World A: the office (generated, Kling 3.0 Pro) | World B: the product (real UI, deterministic compositing) |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Photoreal, anamorphic feel, shallow depth of field, warm tungsten practicals + cool window light, slight haze | Clean white/near-black stage, soft gradient, precise shadows, Apple-style |
| Grade | Teal-amber, lifted blacks in the chaos scenes; cleaner and brighter after the transformation | Neutral, true-to-brand cyan → violet accents |
| Camera | Smooth Steadicam follows, slow push-ins, one dolly-zoom moment | Slow 3D push-ins on a device, gentle parallax, kinetic type |

## The thought monster ("Knot")
- **Body:** a small, floating tangle of black scribble threads with colour flecks (blue, red, yellow, green), big pale-green eyes with tiny pupils, little stick arms and legs. It comes from the supplied character sheet (brand/monster-sheet.webp), rendered as a **3D, Pixar-meets-stop-motion creature** so it sits in a photoreal office.
- **Effects:** emits faint glowing threads and question-mark / lightning glyphs when stressed.
- **Personality:** curious, clumsy, a little mischievous, never scary.
- **Arc:** tangled → rides on the CEO's head → strands straighten into the aperture logo's six blades → the logo.

## The CEO ("Daniel")
Early 40s, owner of a small equipment-rental and service company. Rolled sleeves, open-collar navy shirt, tired eyes, short dark hair with grey at the temples, light stubble. Approachable, not a suit.

## The company
A mid-size, slightly worn modern office in a brick-and-glass building. Four spaces reused before and after:
1. Front desk / admin
2. Scheduling wall
3. Finance corner
4. Open floor

## Typography (World B)
- **Font:** Poppins SemiBold, large, white on near-black or ink on white.
- **Motion:** words fade and slide in 12 px over 0.4 s with ease-out; one idea per card.

## Sound
- **Opening:** dark, playful pizzicato and soft synth pad.
- **Chaos:** rising ticking percussion.
- **Pause:** silence on the realization beat.
- **Generation:** airy arpeggio swell.
- **Showcase:** confident, minimal electronic pulse.
- **Transformation:** cinematic crescendo.
- **End:** single resolved chord and a logo "click-shimmer" stinger.

## Honesty rules
- **Real UI only:** every UI moment is a real recording.
- **No fake integrations:** no logos of tools we don't support. The calendar feed and webhooks are shown as generic icons plus the names Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar.
- **No speed promises:** "minutes, not months" at most.
- **No real payment:** never show a card form; the checkout is a demo.
